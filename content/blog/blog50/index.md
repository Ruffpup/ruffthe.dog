In the last few weeks, I’ve had to say this more times than I should ever have to: I teach about sex, and you still need to ask before you send me sexts, dick pics, or ass pics. I think that some people assume that because I am a sex educator who teaches workshops on various kink and sex-adjascent topics, that it’s a green light for them to message me asking if I’m top or bottom, sending sexy pics or just being downright crass. Consent is negotiated, ongoing, and should never be implied.

I generally give people 3 strikes, once as a friendly reminder, second as a stern warning and third equals a block.

He sent me explicit pictures I hadn’t asked for. I politely told him I wasn’t interested in that kind of interaction with him. He apologized, and we continued to chat. A few minutes later he was asking about what position I was and how often I would engage in sex. I again reminded him more sternly that I was not comfortable discussing my sexual activities with him and that if he continued I would be ending the conversation. He again apologized and I didn’t hear any of it for several more days. But then, immediately he started the conversation talking about masturbation and I promptly told him that I had warned him several times and that I would be blocking him for not respecting my boundaries.

I hate to do that because it is a very fine line between teaching about kink and sharing my experiences, and talking in detail about my play sessions. It’s jarring to get a notification that you’ve gotten a message from someone, only to open the message and see dick and ass that I wasn’t ready for.

What people so often understand is that even behind the scenes of my classes, my sharing of experience is very carefully planned and there are levels of consent within that process as well. Firstly, I never share anything that I have not explicitly asked the other parties involved if I can share. I preface that I don’t intend to share names or specific details, but there may be elements that could be identifiable. For example, with my gunge play class there are only a few people who have played with me in that context. If I share information about those scenes anyone who has any context about who might be in town or when I may have had a scene could potentially know who the other parties were.

I asked someone I’d played with if he’d be okay with me sharing my experience as the sub in a gunge scene. I told him I’d leave out his name, but that people might still be able to figure out who he was. Having been in that position, I could speak from experience about what a sub might feel in that space. It also helped me better understand what to watch for to keep the scene safe, how to prepare the materials, and how to dispose of them during cleanup.

When I plan my classes I will build those agreed to examples into the coursework and that’s the extent that I share those specific details. I can still be very kandid about kink, but still decide how much of my sex life I want to discuss, with whom and when.

The best way to approach me is to have a conversation with me. It can be as simple as, “I really enjoyed your class. Would you be open to talking more about what we covered?” If you want to ask me something about my own sex life, try, “Can I ask you a personal question about sex?” And if you want to send me an explicit picture, ask me if I’d like to see it before you send it.

Then give me a chance to answer. If I say no, take me at my word. If I don’t answer, leave it there. I’m glad you feel comfortable talking to me about sex. I want to feel comfortable in that conversation, too.
